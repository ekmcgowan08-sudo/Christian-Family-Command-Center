## 2. The Business Model (Digital Product Flywheel)

**Starting niche:** Faith & Family Home Management — underserved, high emotional urgency, repeat buyers, plays directly to existing Google Sheets skill.

**The loop:** Traffic (Pinterest) → Email List (ConvertKit) → Digital Product Portfolio (Payhip + Gumroad) → Reinvest & Compound.

**Product ladder:**

| Level | Product | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Weekly Family Reset Checklist | $0 |
| Entry | Prayer & Weekly Planner Printable | $9 |
| Core | Christian Family Command Center (Google Sheets) | $29 |
| Bundle | Budget + Giving + Meal Planning Bundle | $49 |
| Premium | Complete Faith & Family Operating System | $99 |

**Honest timeline:** $0–200/mo (months 1–3) → $200–1,000 (months 4–6) → $1,000–4,000 (months 7–12) → $4,000–8,000 (months 13–24) → $8,000–12,000 (months 24–36). First 90 days will likely produce little revenue — that is expected, not a failure signal.

## 5. First 90 Days — Milestone Targets

- 8–10 digital products live
- 150+ Pinterest pins published
- 200–800 email subscribers
- First $100–$500/month revenue

**Validation gates to check weekly:** product page conversion (1–3%), Pinterest pin click rate (1–2%+), email opt-in rate (2–5%), email open rate (30%+ on welcome sequence). If underperforming, revise the offer/design/copy before adding new channels.

## 7. Guardrails Carried Over from Master Project Bible

- Do not quit any current income source until 6 consecutive months at target revenue, a 6-month emergency fund exists, income is diversified across products/traffic sources, health insurance is settled, and a tax professional has been consulted.
- Set aside 25–30% of gross revenue for taxes.
- Measure profit, not vanity metrics (views/followers).
- Favor products solving a specific, painful problem for a clearly defined buyer.
- Build the ministry-adjacent product line first before expanding into unrelated niches — this uses existing credibility and reduces fragmentation, consistent with the portfolio-wide decision already logged in the Master Project Bible.
