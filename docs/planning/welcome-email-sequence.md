**Subject line options:**

- "One small shift that made our week calmer"

**Body:**

Hi [First Name],

Quick tip today: if your week feels chaotic by Wednesday, it might not be a scheduling problem — it might be a Sunday problem.

Try this: every Sunday evening, spend just 10 minutes doing three things:
1. Glance at the week ahead (school, work, appointments)
2. Pick one Scripture or prayer focus for the week
3. Write down your top 3 priorities — not 10, just 3

That's it. It sounds small, but it's the difference between reacting to your week and leading it.
